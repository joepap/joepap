# Help Table — Worker Guide

*IAFF Local 36 · one page, print this*

You handle everyone the main check-in tables can't finish on their own.
Open **`<server-link>/discrepancy.html`** on your device (the address says
"discrepancy" — it opens the Help Table), enter the admin PIN. Or use the
**Help Table tab inside the Admin page** — same queue, same PIN.
Keep the **printed payroll list** (and the original scanned report) at your table.

## Who lands in your queue, and what to do

**🟢 "GOT BALLOT · GET CONTACT" — data record** (checked in, got a ballot, just needs contact)
1. These people are **already done voting** — they have their ballot. Nothing to
   decide.
2. They're in NEP but have **no online portal account**. Get a **personal email**
   (NOT @dc.gov — the form rejects those) and/or phone so we can invite them to
   the members portal after the meeting.
3. Type it in → **✓ Save contact & done**. If they won't give info, **No info —
   done**. Either way it's logged.

**🔴 "NEEDS REVIEW"** (an NEP member who isn't on the payroll dues list)
1. Look them up on the **printed payroll list** (it's alphabetical).
2. **Found on paper?** The scan missed them → tap their name → **✓ Override —
   issue ballot**. Done.
3. **Not on paper either?** They may have promoted out of the unit, pay some other
   way, or be behind on dues. Ask; use judgment or wave over the E-board.
   Ballot → **Override**. No ballot → **No ballot** (logged).

**🟡 payroll-only** (paying dues via payroll but not in the NEP system)
1. Confirmed dues-payers — they **get a ballot**.
2. Ask for their **personal email** (not @dc.gov) and phone; type them in.
3. **✓ Override — issue ballot**, then hand them a **registration QR card**.

**🔴 "NON DUES-PAYING MEMBER"** (on the union's known non-payer list)
- No ballot unless they can show they've started paying (E-board call).
- If cleared: **Override**; otherwise **No ballot**.

## Walk-ups — no need to send them away

If someone walks up who **isn't** in your list yet, use the **"Look up anyone"**
search box at the top: type their last name (or member #), tap their name, and
they drop into your queue so you can resolve them right there. The queue entry
still records the paper trail.

Someone who's in **no list at all** (search finds nothing, not on the paper
payroll either): the main table already logged them as "not on roster" — check
the paper payroll yourself, and wave the E-board over to decide. No ballot
without that call.

## Good to know

- Tap a name to open it; tap again to close. One tap = it opens.
- Every decision is logged with the time — the post-vote paper trail.
- **Undo**: resolved the wrong person? Tell the admin — a wrongly issued ballot
  can be voided from the admin dashboard.
- The queue updates live; people appear as main tables send them.
- More than one device can work the queue at once.
- Links to the Admin and Check-in screens are in the top bar.
