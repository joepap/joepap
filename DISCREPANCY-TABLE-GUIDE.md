# Discrepancy Table — Worker Guide

*IAFF Local 36 · one page, print this*

You resolve everyone the main check-in tables can't issue a ballot to.
Open **`<server-link>/discrepancy.html`** on your device, enter the table PIN.
Keep the **printed payroll list** (and the original scanned report) at your table.

## Who lands in your queue, and what to do

**🔴 RED — "NOT ON DUES PAYROLL"** (an NEP member who isn't on the payroll dues list)
1. Look them up on the **printed payroll list** (it's alphabetical).
2. **Found on paper?** The scan missed them → tap their name in the queue →
   **✓ Override — issue ballot**. Done.
3. **Not on paper either?** They may have promoted out of the unit, pay some other
   way, or be behind on dues. Ask; use judgment or wave over the E-board.
   Ballot → **Override**. No ballot → **No ballot** (logged).

**🟡 YELLOW — "payroll-only"** (paying dues via payroll but not in the NEP system)
1. They're confirmed dues-payers — they **get a ballot**.
2. Before tapping: ask for their **personal email** (NOT @dc.gov — the form
   rejects those) and phone. Type them in.
3. Tap **✓ Override — issue ballot**, then hand them a **registration QR card**
   so they join the members section.

**🔴 "NON DUES-PAYING MEMBER"** (on the union's known non-payer list)
- No ballot unless they can show they've started paying (E-board call).
- If cleared: **Override**; otherwise **No ballot**.

## Good to know

- Every decision is logged with your name/time — the post-vote paper trail.
- **Undo**: if you resolve the wrong person, tell the admin — a wrongly issued
  ballot check-in can be voided from the admin dashboard.
- The queue updates live; people appear as main tables send them.
- More than one device can work the queue at once.
- If someone walks up who isn't in your queue, send them back to a main
  check-in table first — the queue entry is what keeps the paper trail.
